## Projects

**[Fed for a Fiver](https://github.com/oliviagallego/fiver)** · [fedforafiver.com](https://fedforafiver.com)
Where to eat in London under £5, open now, at the price you actually pay once your student cards are applied. You photograph a menu, AI vision reads the prices, and the map grows without anyone typing anything. Built it on my own. Second place at the Claude Campus Hackathon, 2026.

**[TesaHealth](https://github.com/oliviagallego/TesaHealth)**
My bachelor thesis, graded 8.5/10. A patient describes new symptoms, AI drafts a clinical vignette and a differential with an urgency level, and several doctors review it blind until they agree. If any one of them says it's urgent, urgent is what stands. Tested with 20 patients and clinicians.

**[Medica Nova: lung CT malignancy classification](https://github.com/oliviagallego/medica-nova-lung-ct)**
Radiomics and a tuned, probability-calibrated Random Forest on 2,300 CT scans. It beat the CNN, the ResNet and the EfficientNet we tried, which was not what we expected on a dataset this small and this imbalanced. F1 and ROC AUC both around 0.84. Team of three.

**[RedVital](https://github.com/oliviagallego/red-vital)**
Hospitals post a blood request and compatible donors nearby get notified. Six of us built it; I coordinated the team and wrote the authentication and the request flow.

**[Blood cells classification](https://github.com/oliviagallego/an2dl-blood-cells-classification)** · EfficientNet. I spent most of the time on augmentation, trying to make it hold up on images it had not seen.

**[Martian terrain segmentation](https://github.com/oliviagallego/an2dl-martian-segmentation)** · U-Net. Mostly a lesson in class imbalance.

**[ObstetricianClinic](https://github.com/oliviagallego/ObstetricianClinic)** · Database design, ERD to normalised schema.
